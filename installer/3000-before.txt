FIPinator 3000 is the gauge maker for FIPinator: start from a template, build an airspeed gauge from
your aircraft's speeds, combine gauges, change their look, and tweak them. It's free and open source
(AGPL-3.0).

It's optional: FIPinator Live already comes with all the ready-made gauges. Install FIPinator 3000 only
if you want to make or change gauges. You don't need a FIP or the sim to use it (reading speeds from the
sim needs MSFS 2024 running).

FIPinator 3000 can also save your own gauges for SPAD.neXt.

Installs for your Windows user only (no administrator rights). The gauges you make are kept in
Documents\FIPinator 3000\Gauges, and uninstalling never deletes them.

Made for Windows 11.
